Abhinay G

I’m Abhinay, a final-year BSc Computer Science student at Reva University in Bengaluru, specializing in Cloud Computing and Big Data. I work mostly with machine learning and data: I’ve clustered Premier League players by playing style, built a chess app with a Stockfish-backed best-move indicator, and analyzed housing data to see what actually drives prices. Before and during my degree I taught computer science and science to grades 3 to 10 and worked on AI data labeling at CloudFactory. Both taught me to explain things clearly and to care about data quality. I’m also part of the organizing team at Google Developers Group at Reva, where we run hackathons and workshops. Outside of code, I play electric guitar (mostly rock and metal) and follow football closely. This site is built around both. I’m looking to move into ML and data science work. If you have something in that space, get in touch.
